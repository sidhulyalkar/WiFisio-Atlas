# Research roadmap

## Stage A: software validity

Complete when schemas, adapters, calibration, deterministic runs, baselines,
nulls, uncertainty, provenance, and fault injection pass. v0.3 targets this
stage.

## Stage B: measurement validity

Collect synchronized RF and reference sensors. Quantify clock error, geometry
error, signal quality, motion sensitivity, and repeatability before optimizing
models.

## Stage C: physiological validity

Demonstrate subject-, day-, and room-held-out waveform recovery that beats
simple baselines and nulls. Evaluate abstention and uncertainty calibration.

## Stage D: regional attribution

Use pose, mmWave, or ultrasound supervision to test one body region and one
measured displacement at a time. Require cross-geometry replication.

## Stage E: translational validation

Define a preregistered use case, independent test cohort, reference device,
error tolerance, safety analysis, and governance plan. This stage is outside
the claims of the current repository.
